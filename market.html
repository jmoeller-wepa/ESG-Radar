Updated by the scheduled GitHub Action; most statistics are published monthly with a delay of one to three months, energy prices half-yearly, industry figures yearly. Indices are for trend orientation only. Exchange-quoted pulp and recovered-paper prices (FOEX/Fastmarkets PIX, EUWID) are paid data and therefore not shown.
